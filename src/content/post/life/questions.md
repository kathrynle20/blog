During my conversations, I am always consciously thinking about what topics I should bring up. In a moment of silence, I find myself thinking about what questions to ask and what direction to continue the conversation in. A helpful way that I think about questions is in categories including work/school, hobbies/interests, family, and relationships.

## Work/School
My goto conversation topic that I normally start with is asking about work/school. Since most people spend most of their time doing either work or school, it is a very easy topic to ask about. Especially when I don't know a lot about a person, it is a great way to start learning more about their academic/work interests and what they spend most of their time working on. Some questions I would ask include:
- What do you find most interesting about your work/school?
- What classes are you taking/have taken/plan on taking?
- What was your (least) favorite class?
- What are you thinking about at work/school currently?
